# DREAM BRANCH — v3.3-dream (flagged, not crowned)

Form 7 (Dream-State Consolidation) is the engine's most original organ:
most memory systems retrieve; this one COMPOSES — it makes new memories
out of old ones. This branch tunes its ear. The architecture was never
the problem; the scoring was.

## The conviction (v3.2, measured)
Raw-cosine resonance cannot tell a rare shared concept from shared
filler words: 99 bridges, precision 0.030, planted insights ranked
255/700 (mid-pack). Rarity was provably the signal the ear ignored —
planted bridges' shared terms mean IDF 1.79 vs flukes 0.77.

## The three levers (this branch)
1. **IDF-weighted resonance** — serendipity is sharing RARE structure.
2. **Dream budget** (default 4/cycle) — dreams are rare on purpose;
   repeated cycles surface the rest; floods are impossible by
   construction.
3. **Rare-anchor gate** (df <= dream_rare_anchor_df, default 2) — no
   bridge without at least one genuinely uncommon shared term.

A tuned Form 7 still hears only LEXICAL serendipity — shared rare
words. Connections that share zero vocabulary (storm-inversion <->
breath-rhythm class insights) need a semantic proposer: a local model
suggesting bridges, the engine receipting and storing them. That is
P2 of the Sovereign Node map — engine ports into the Governor, the
resident Brain serves — and this branch's bridge storage/retrieval/
receipt layer is exactly what that future dreamer plugs into. Nothing
built here is throwaway.
